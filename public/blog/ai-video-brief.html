Give an AI video a clear job before choosing a style.

A video is more useful when it answers one audience question and points to one next step.

By Zanove Technologies

A video is more useful when it answers one audience question and points to one next step.

Write the message in one sentence

Decide who the video is for, what they should understand and what action makes sense afterwards. That brief keeps the visuals, narration, captions and call to action working together.

Build the human review into the process

Review names, claims, brand details, image rights and pronunciation before publishing. AI tools can speed up drafts and production, but a person should check that the final result is accurate, recognisable and appropriate for the audience.

Then adapt the piece to where it will appear. A short vertical edit, a website explainer and a product walkthrough each need different pacing and context. Reuse the core idea while shaping each version for its job.

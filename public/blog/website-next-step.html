Before you redesign your website, make the next step obvious.

A fresh look can help, but visitors still need to understand what you do and what to do next.

By Zanove Technologies

A fresh look can help, but visitors still need to understand what you do and what to do next.

Start with the visitor’s question

Ask someone unfamiliar with your business to open the homepage for a few seconds. Can they tell who the service is for, what problem it solves and how to get help? If those answers are hidden in long paragraphs or scattered across the page, the redesign brief should start with clarity.

Trace one useful journey

Pick a high-value visitor, such as a potential client looking for a specific service. Follow their path from the first page to an enquiry. Note where they need more proof, where the language feels vague and where a button or contact detail is hard to find.

That small journey map helps a team spend design and development effort on the parts that make a decision easier. A website should look like your business and guide people through it.
